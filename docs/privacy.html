Archopy · qupix

Privacy

Effective 26 November 2026

On your PC

Archopy stores preferences, widget positions, copies of imported images and lyric files under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Archopy. Enabling startup adds an entry for Archopy in your Windows user account. Removing a photo widget hides the widget. Imported files can remain in the local folder.

Spotify sign-in

Spotify handles sign-in in your browser. With your permission, Archopy reads playback state, downloads artwork and sends playback commands directly to Spotify. An access token is held in memory. A refresh token protected by Windows for your user account allows sign-in to survive a restart.

Lyrics lookup

Enabling Desktop lyrics lets Archopy send the current track title, artist, album and duration to LRCLIB to find synchronized lyrics. A matching local lyric file takes priority. Online lyrics are held in memory. Spotify credentials are used only with Spotify. External services receive ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Your controls

Disable Desktop lyrics to stop lookups. Choose Disconnect in Archopy to delete the saved Spotify session. You can also revoke access in Spotify account settings. To erase all local preferences and imports, exit Archopy and remove its local data folder. Archopy has no analytics service or developer-operated backend.
